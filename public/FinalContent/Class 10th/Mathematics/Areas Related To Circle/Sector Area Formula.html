Welcome to the derivation of the sector area formula! We'll use the unitary method to build the formula step by step.
🎯 Starting Point

We begin with what we know about circles:

Full Circle Area = πr²
Full Circle Angle = 360°

These are our fundamental building blocks for the derivation.

🔢 Unitary Method

The unitary method helps us find proportional relationships:

If 360° → πr²
Then 1° → πr²/360
Then θ° → (θ/360) × πr²

This logical progression leads us to our final formula.

🏆 Final Formula

The complete sector area formula:

Area = (θ/360) × πr²

Where θ is the angle in degrees and r is the radius. This formula gives us the exact area of any sector!
