The Origin - Bone Marrow Factory

Where blood cells are born
Journey Progress 0%
Origin Blood Heart Vessels Circulation Exchange Defense Problems
Deep within your bones lies a remarkable factory - the bone marrow. This spongy tissue is where your blood's incredible journey begins. Every second, millions of new blood cells are born here, ready to embark on their life-sustaining missions throughout your body.
🔬 Key Concepts
  • Red marrow produces all blood cells
  • 2 million red blood cells created per second
  • Stem cells differentiate into specialized cells
  • Found in flat bones and long bone ends
Amazing Fact!
Your bone marrow produces about 500 billion blood cells every day - that's more than the number of stars in our galaxy!

By exploring this section, you'll understand how blood cells are created and why bone marrow is essential for life.
