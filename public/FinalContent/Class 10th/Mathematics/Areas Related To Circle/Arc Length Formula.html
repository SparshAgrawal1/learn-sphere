Welcome to the derivation of the arc length formula! We'll use the unitary method to understand how arc length relates to circumference.
🎯 Starting Point

We begin with what we know about circle circumference:

Circumference = 2πr
Full Circle = 360°

The circumference is the total length around the circle's edge.

📐 Unitary Method

Using proportional reasoning for arc lengths:

If 360° → 2πr
Then 1° → 2πr/360
Then θ° → (θ/360) × 2πr

This creates our step-by-step logical progression.

🏆 Final Formula

The complete arc length formula:

Arc Length = (θ/360) × 2πr

Where θ is the angle in degrees and r is the radius. This gives us the exact length of any arc!
