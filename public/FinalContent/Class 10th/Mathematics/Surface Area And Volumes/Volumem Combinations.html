Volume of Combination of Solids

Understanding Space Inside Combined Shapes

Welcome! Learn how to calculate volumes when solids combine.
🎯 Key Concept
Total Volume = Sum of Parts!

When solids combine, total volume equals the sum of individual volumes. No part disappears!

📐 Formula Strategy
V = V₁ + V₂ + V₃...

Calculate each solid's volume separately, then add them together.

🔍 Common Formulas
Cylinder: πr²h
Cone: (1/3)πr²h
Hemisphere: (2/3)πr³

Remember these key volume formulas for combinations.
