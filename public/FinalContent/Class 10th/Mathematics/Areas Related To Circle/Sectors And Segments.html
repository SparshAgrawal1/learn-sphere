Welcome to Sectors and Segments of a Circle! Learn how circles can be divided into meaningful geometric regions.
🍰 Sector

The portion of a circular region enclosed by two radii and the corresponding arc. Think of it like a slice of pie - bounded by two straight edges (radii) and one curved edge (arc). Can be either minor (smaller) or major (larger).

Region OAPB with radii OA, OB
🌙 Segment

The portion of a circular region enclosed between a chord and the corresponding arc. It's what remains when you "cut off" part of a circle with a straight line. Can be either minor (smaller) or major (larger).
