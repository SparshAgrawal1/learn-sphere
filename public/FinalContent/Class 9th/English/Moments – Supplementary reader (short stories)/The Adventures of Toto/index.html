🌟 Welcome to "The Adventures of Toto"

This story takes us into the world of animals and their relationship with humans. It's a humorous tale by Ruskin Bond about a mischievous monkey named Toto and his adventures with the narrator's grandfather, who has a private zoo at home.

In this interactive lesson, we'll explore Ruskin Bond's storytelling through the eyes of the narrator, who witnesses the amusing and sometimes destructive antics of Toto the monkey, and the challenges of keeping such a wild and intelligent creature as a pet.

📝 Let's Begin

Have you ever had a pet or wanted to have one? What kind of animal would you like to have as a pet?

The story we're about to read is about a monkey named Toto who becomes a pet. Monkeys are known to be mischievous and intelligent animals. Let's think about what makes them fascinating but also challenging as pets.

Pre-Reading Activity: Pets and Their Behavior

Think about these questions and share your thoughts:

1. What qualities make an animal a good pet?

2. Why do you think keeping wild animals as pets might be problematic?

3. Have you ever observed monkeys? What behaviors did you notice?
