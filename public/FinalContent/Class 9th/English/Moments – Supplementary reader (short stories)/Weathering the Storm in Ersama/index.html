🌟 Welcome to "Weathering the Storm in Ersama"

This story takes us through the devastating impact of a super cyclone that hit Orissa in 1999 and the remarkable resilience shown by a young man named Prashant and his community in the aftermath of this natural disaster.

In this interactive lesson, we'll explore Harsh Mander's powerful narrative about how human determination, leadership, and community spirit can help people recover from even the most catastrophic events.

📝 Let's Begin

Natural disasters like cyclones, floods, earthquakes, and tsunamis cause enormous damage to life and property. The survivors face many hardships in rebuilding their lives.

People often come together to help one another in such situations, showing how communities can respond to crises with resilience and compassion.

Pre-Reading Activity: Responding to Natural Disasters

Think about a natural disaster that you have experienced, read about, or seen on television. What kind of problems do people face during and after such calamities?

In your opinion, what qualities are needed in a person to effectively lead a community through a natural disaster? What role can young people play in helping communities during such crises?
