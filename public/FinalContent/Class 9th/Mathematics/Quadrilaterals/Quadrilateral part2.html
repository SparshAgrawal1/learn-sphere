📚 Key Concepts & Theory

📐 Mid-point Theorem

8.8

Statement: The line segment joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and equal to half of it.

If D and E are mid-points of AB and AC respectively,

then DE ∥ BC and DE = ½ BC

🔄 Converse Theorem

8.9

Statement: The line drawn through the mid-point of one side of a triangle and parallel to another side bisects the third side.

If D is mid-point of AB and DE ∥ BC,

then E is mid-point of AC

🔑 Key Properties

⭐
  • Mid-point line is parallel to third side
  • Mid-point line is half the length of third side
  • Works for any triangle
  • Creates similar triangles

🔬 Proof Strategy

📝
  1. Extend EF to point D
  2. Join CD (construction)
  3. Prove △AEF ≅ △CDF
  4. Show BECD is parallelogram
  5. Conclude EF ∥ BC and EF = ½ BC
