Welcome to Real-World Probability Applications! Explore how probability theory applies to everyday situations through interactive examples and simulations.
🃏 Card Probability

Drawing cards from decks involves calculating favorable outcomes versus total possibilities. Essential for understanding conditional probability and independent events.

P(Event) = Favorable Cards / Total Cards
👥 Selection Problems

Random selection from groups with different characteristics. Applications include class representatives, jury selection, and sampling processes.

P(Selection) = Group Size / Total Population
🔍 Quality Control

Defective item detection in manufacturing. Critical for business decisions, quality assurance, and customer satisfaction management.

P(Defective) = Defective Items / Total Items
🔴 Marble Problems

Multi-colored object selection from containers. Fundamental probability scenarios that build intuition for more complex problems.

P(Color) = Same Color Marbles / Total Marbles
